Lighting up cities since 2004

Christmas Decor has been operating since 2004 in Karakule near Supraśl, close to Białystok in north-eastern Poland. We supply cities, shopping centres, squares, boulevards and parks with decorative LED lighting for every occasion.

We design and make our decorations ourselves, install and service them. Our illuminations shine in towns in Poland and abroad, and we present new designs at the Christmasworld fair in Frankfurt.
